---
description: List tasks, optionally filtered by status
---

Treat `$ARGUMENTS` as the filter clause of the project-tasks skill's
`list tasks` trigger — empty `$ARGUMENTS` means "list tasks" (pending and
in_progress only); `all` means every status except cancelled; a status word
(e.g. `completed`) means "list tasks completed".

Follow the project-tasks skill's instructions for that trigger.
